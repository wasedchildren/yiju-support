Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new entry or task?

Open YiJu and use the main creation action on the home screen to add a new item, then save it to your daily list or schedule.

Why are notifications not appearing?

Make sure notifications are enabled for YiJu in your iPhone Settings. If reminders still do not appear, send a message with your iOS version and a short description of the problem.

How can I report a bug or request a feature?

Email e_qiujun@enn.cn with the subject line YiJu Support. Screenshots and short reproduction steps are very helpful.
